S037 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 4 | Question Summary

S039 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 8 | Response
S040 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 9 | The City's priorities were established through a discovery process that included staff engagement, public engagement, and review by a cross-departmental working group.
S041 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 11 | The primary priorities identified for the project are:
S042 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 12 | 1. Find Information (Navigation & Search)
S043 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 13 | 2. Ease of Site Use (Mobile, Usability, Accessibility)
S044 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 14 | 3. Easily Complete Processes (Forms, Payments, Processes)
S045 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 15 | Additional priorities include:
S046 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 16 | 4. Clearly Identify Help (Contact & Routing)
S047 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 17 | 5. Access Public Information (Meetings, Documents, Transparency)
S048 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 18 | 6. Accurate & Timely Content
S049 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 19 | The City seeks measurable improvements in usability, accessibility, service completion, public information access, and content management.
S050 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 19 | Vendors are encouraged to recommend metrics, analytics approaches, and ongoing optimization strategies that support these objectives.
S051 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 23 | 2. Content Migration and Governance
S052 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 24 | Question Summary
S053 | wc-addendum1 p. 2 line 25 | Several vendors requested clarification regarding the amount of content to be migrated, content ownership, migration responsibilities, PDF conversion, and governance expectations.
S054 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 1 | Response
S055 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 2 | The City has not completed a final content inventory.
S056 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 2 | A preliminary review identified 125 currently active (published) web pages, and 600 documents were uploaded to the website during 2025, such as agendas, minutes, and forms.
S057 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 5 | The City anticipates migrating approximately 75 pages and 700 documents to the new website.
S058 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 5 | These documents include an estimated 490 agendas, minutes, and meeting packets beginning January 1, 2026, based on 2025 document counts.
S059 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 8 | The selected vendor will be expected to assist the City in developing a content inventory, migration strategy, and governance framework during implementation.
S060 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 10 | As stated in the RFP, the City anticipates migrating active content and approximately the past year of archived content to the new platform.
S061 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 10 | Final migration decisions will be made collaboratively during implementation.
S062 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 10 | Vendors should include a content inventory and migration planning process within their proposed approach.
S063 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 14 | The City anticipates participation from multiple departments in reviewing content, identifying outdated information, and determining migration priorities.
S064 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 16 | The reference to "content ownership" in the RFP relates to establishing accountability for content maintenance, updates, review cycles, publishing permissions, and long-term governance responsibilities.
S065 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 19 | Additional Clarification – PDF Content
S066 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 20 | The City recognizes that document accessibility and usability are important project considerations.
S067 | wc-addendum1 p. 3 line 20 | Vendors are encouraged to recommend practical approaches for improving accessibility, searchability, and long-term management of document-based content, including strategies that may reduce reliance on PDFs over time.
