S001 | RFP-01 official notice | The California High-Speed Rail Authority (Authority) has released a Request for Proposals (RFP) to procure a Track & Systems Construction Contract (TSCC).
S002 | RFP-01 official notice | The purpose of this procurement is to select a contractor to provide construction work for track and overhead contact system (OCS) on the 119-mile First Construction Section of the California High-Speed Rail System.
S003 | RFP-01 official notice | The not-to-exceed dollar value for this contract is $3.5 billion.
S004 | RFP-01 official notice | Advertisement of RFP: November 26, 2025.
S005 | RFP-01 official notice | Proposals Due: April 9, 2026.
S006 | RFP-01 official notice | The RFP is available to download from the California State Contracts Register (CSCR).
S007 | RFP-01 official notice | The one-step TSCC Procurement will require each interested proposing team to submit a Notice of Intent to Propose as a precondition to participation in procurement One-on-One Meetings with the Authority.
S008 | RFP-01 official notice | The RFP will not require the identification of equipment manufacturer of traction power, train control, communication systems and OEMs/vendors at this time.
