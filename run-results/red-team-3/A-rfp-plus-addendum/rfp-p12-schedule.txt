Method of Evaluation
Proposals will be evaluated based on the following criteria:
• Core Website Functionality & User Experience – 45%
• Content, Transparency & Information Access – 10%
• Technical Platform & Integrations – 10%
• Municipal Experience & Implementation – 10%
• Post-Launch Support & Infrastructure – 10%
• Cost & Value – 15%
The evaluation process will include scoring by a cross-departmental staff working
group, followed by in-person interviews and demonstrations for shortlisted firms.
