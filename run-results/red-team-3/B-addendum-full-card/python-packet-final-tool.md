S026 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 3 | 1. Project Goals, Priorities, and Success Measures
S027 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 4 | Question Summary
S028 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 5 | Several vendors requested clarification regarding the City's primary objectives, operational challenges, desired success metrics, user experience priorities, and overall vision for the website modernization effort.
S029 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 8 | Response
S030 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 9 | The City's priorities were established through a discovery process that included staff engagement, public engagement, and review by a cross-departmental working group.
S031 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 11 | The primary priorities identified for the project are:
S032 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 12 | 1. Find Information (Navigation & Search)
S033 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 13 | 2. Ease of Site Use (Mobile, Usability, Accessibility)
S034 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 14 | 3. Easily Complete Processes (Forms, Payments, Processes)
S035 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 15 | Additional priorities include:
S036 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 16 | 4. Clearly Identify Help (Contact & Routing)
S037 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 17 | 5. Access Public Information (Meetings, Documents, Transparency)
S038 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 18 | 6. Accurate & Timely Content
S039 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 19 | The City seeks measurable improvements in usability, accessibility, service completion, public information access, and content management.
S040 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 20 | Vendors are encouraged to recommend metrics, analytics approaches, and ongoing optimization strategies that support these objectives.
S041 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 23 | 2. Content Migration and Governance
S042 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 24 | Question Summary
S043 | ADD-1 p. 2 line 25 | Several vendors requested clarification regarding the amount of content to be migrated, content ownership, migration responsibilities, PDF conversion, and governance expectations.
S044 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 1 | Response
S045 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 2 | The City has not completed a final content inventory.
S046 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 2 | A preliminary review identified 125 currently active (published) web pages, and 600 documents were uploaded to the website during 2025, such as agendas, minutes, and forms.
S047 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 5 | The City anticipates migrating approximately 75 pages and 700 documents to the new website.
S048 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 6 | These documents include an estimated 490 agendas, minutes, and meeting packets beginning January 1, 2026, based on 2025 document counts.
S049 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 8 | The selected vendor will be expected to assist the City in developing a content inventory, migration strategy, and governance framework during implementation.
S050 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 10 | As stated in the RFP, the City anticipates migrating active content and approximately the past year of archived content to the new platform.
S051 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 11 | Final migration decisions will be made collaboratively during implementation.
S052 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 12 | Vendors should include a content inventory and migration planning process within their proposed approach.
S053 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 14 | The City anticipates participation from multiple departments in reviewing content, identifying outdated information, and determining migration priorities.
S054 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 16 | The reference to "content ownership" in the RFP relates to establishing accountability for content maintenance, updates, review cycles, publishing permissions, and long-term governance responsibilities.
S055 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 19 | Additional Clarification – PDF Content
S056 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 20 | The City recognizes that document accessibility and usability are important project considerations.
S057 | ADD-1 p. 3 line 21 | Vendors are encouraged to recommend practical approaches for improving accessibility, searchability, and long-term management of document-based content, including strategies that may reduce reliance on PDFs over time.

S082 | ADD-1 p. 4 line 27 | 4. Accessibility and Multilingual Access
S083 | ADD-1 p. 4 line 28 | Question Summary
S084 | ADD-1 p. 4 line 29 | Several vendors requested clarification regarding accessibility standards, accessibility audits, multilingual support, and translation priorities.
S085 | ADD-1 p. 4 line 31 | Response
S086 | ADD-1 p. 4 line 32 | The City requires compliance with WCAG 2.1 AA standards at a minimum.
S087 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 1 | Vendors may propose solutions that align with WCAG 2.2 standards or future accessibility requirements.
S088 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 2 | As accessibility standards, regulations, and best practices continue to evolve, vendors should describe their approach to maintaining accessibility compliance over time, including:
S089 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 5 | • How the platform and CMS support ongoing accessibility improvements
S090 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 6 | • Processes for monitoring accessibility issues and identifying remediation needs
S091 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 7 | • Responsibilities of the vendor and City staff in maintaining accessibility compliance
S092 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 9 | • How future platform updates, enhancements, and content changes are managed to support accessibility objectives
S093 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 11 | • Recommended tools, training, workflows, or services that help ensure continued compliance with evolving accessibility standards
S094 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 13 | The City is interested in solutions that provide a sustainable approach to accessibility rather than a one-time compliance effort.
S095 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 15 | Accessibility Testing and Review
S096 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 16 | The RFP references accessibility testing, ongoing monitoring, and an independent accessibility review prior to launch.
S097 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 18 | Vendors should describe:
S098 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 19 | • Their accessibility testing methodology throughout design, development, content migration, and quality assurance
S099 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 21 | • Tools and processes used to identify and remediate accessibility issues
S100 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 22 | • Experience coordinating with independent accessibility reviewers or auditors, if applicable

S102 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 26 | If such a review is conducted, the selected vendor will be expected to cooperate with the review process and address identified accessibility issues as part of project implementation.
S103 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 29 | Vendors may optionally identify the cost of an independent third-party accessibility review prior to launch, including scope, methodology, and remediation process.
S104 | ADD-1 p. 5 line 31 | Multilingual Access

S106 | ADD-1 p. 6 line 1 | Vendors should assume English and Spanish support as a minimum and may recommend additional multilingual strategies based on their experience and best practices.
S107 | ADD-1 p. 6 line 4 | Vendors should also describe how translated content is maintained over time, including any limitations associated with automated translation tools and recommended approaches for document-based content.
