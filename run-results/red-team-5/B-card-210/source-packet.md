S011 | NM-EDD p. 4 line 3 | The purpose of the Request for Proposal (RFP) is to solicit sealed proposals to establish a contract through competitive negotiations for support of the New Mexico Economic Development Department Technology and Innovation Office in the design and establishment of funding opportunities from the Research, Development and Deployment Fund.
S012 | NM-EDD p. 4 line 7 | B. BACKGROUND INFORMATION
S013 | NM-EDD p. 4 line 8 | The New Mexico Research, Development and Deployment Fund was created in 2025 for the purpose of catalyzing innovation, economic growth and job creation by providing early-stage capital and financial support to emerging technologies, start-up businesses and research initiatives that align with the state’s economic development plans.
S014 | NM-EDD p. 4 line 11 | The Fund was launched in 2025 with a total balance of $40 million and was appropriated an additional $110 million dollars in 2026, including specific directives of $40 million for quantum initiatives and $40 million for advanced energy initiatives.
