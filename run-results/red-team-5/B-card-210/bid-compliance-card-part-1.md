## 2. Scope, term, and program context stated
- “The purpose of the Request for Proposal (RFP) is to solicit sealed proposals to establish a contract through competitive negotiations for support of the New Mexico Economic Development Department Technology and Innovation Office in the design and establishment of funding opportunities from the Research, Development and Deployment Fund.” [source: source-packet.md:S011; NM-EDD p. 4 line 3]
- “The New Mexico Research, Development and Deployment Fund was created in 2025 for the purpose of catalyzing innovation, economic growth and job creation by providing early-stage capital and financial support to emerging technologies, start-up businesses and research initiatives that align with the state’s economic development plans.” [source: source-packet.md:S013; NM-EDD p. 4 line 8]
- “The Fund was launched in 2025 with a total balance of $40 million and was appropriated an additional $110 million dollars in 2026, including specific directives of $40 million for quantum initiatives and $40 million for advanced energy initiatives.” [source: source-packet.md:S014; NM-EDD p. 4 line 11]
- “This RFP will result in a single award.” [source: source-packet.md:S016; NM-EDD p. 4 line 16]
- “C. Experience with federal grant funding: The contractor should have an understanding of federal grant funding, including relevant nuance, overall timelines, and relevant elements to be considered for the provision of state matching dollars.” [source: source-packet.md:S130; NM-EDD p. 21 line 1]
- “D. Experience with designing and administering large-scale funding opportunities: The contractor should have experience building out large-scale nondilutive funding programs with particular goals and metrics, preferably with experience specifically relating to supporting technology and innovation-based work, including evaluation frameworks, operation procedures for active awards, and performance tracking of awarded funds.” [source: source-packet.md:S131; NM-EDD p. 21 line 4]
